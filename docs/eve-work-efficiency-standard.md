# EVE work-efficiency and durable-progress standard

This standard complements the earn-before-spend policy and owner-approved work loop. It does not lower safety, approval, quota, or spending boundaries.

## Operating principle

**Spend to make progress; preserve the results; never pay twice for the same lesson.** Optimize for expected mission value, not minimum token or neuron use. A costly, well-supported research effort or capability improvement can be justified. Repeated diagnostics, unchanged retries, oversized context, and activity without a reusable result are not.

## Before work

- Read the current task checkpoint and only the prior evidence needed for the next step.
- Identify what is already known, what has already been tried, what failed, and what remains uncertain.
- Reuse recent verified findings when still applicable. Refresh only when facts may have changed or a material uncertainty remains.
- Retrieve targeted excerpts rather than entire histories or large files unless necessary.

## During work

- Keep a concise tried/already-known record: action, outcome, date/source, and whether it should be repeated.
- Use bounded discovery first; deepen research when evidence justifies the added effort.
- Do not repeat a diagnostic or search merely to reconfirm a recently verified result.
- After a failed operation, classify the cause: transient, permanent, unsupported capability, quota/permission, or approach failure.
- Retry only if the cause is plausibly transient and the retry changes or fixes the relevant condition. Never repeat an unchanged failed call. Limit retries to one per distinct failure unless the approved task scope is stricter.
- If blocked, checkpoint the exact blocker and a materially different next step, then yield or stop. Do not loop.

## Closeout and durable learning

Every task closeout should record:
1. Intended outcome.
2. Observed result and supporting evidence.
3. Reusable artifact or location.
4. What is already known and what was tried.
5. Unresolved uncertainty or exact blocker.
6. The next concrete action.

A successful tool call is not automatically a successful task. Activity, token consumption, and a written proposal are not evidence of mission progress by themselves. Prefer verified, reusable deliverables: evidence-backed opportunity briefs, tested prototypes, working capability improvements, or precise blocker reports.

## Resource-value review

Evaluate usage against outcomes, not raw activity alone. Useful measures include:
- Verified deliverables produced.
- Reusable findings/artifacts preserved.
- Repeated diagnostics or duplicate searches avoided.
- Failed retries and their causes.
- Evidence-backed revenue opportunities advanced.
- Working capability improvements delivered.
- Token and neuron estimates, clearly labeled as estimates and not conflated with provider-side account usage.

Do not set a blanket minimum-token target. Spend tokens and neurons when expected mission value justifies them, while respecting local and provider quota guards. Never bypass or raise a guard to continue work.

## Boundaries

This standard grants no additional authority. It does not authorize external contact, publishing, purchases, subscriptions, paid services, quota changes, production writes, deployment, merges, or use of owner devices or credentials. Existing task-level owner approval and all financial/safety rules continue to apply.
